About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Located on Jl. Roti Manis No. 8, Sweet Crumbs Bakery was born out of a genuine devotion to traditional artisanal baking.

We blend classic French viennoiserie with modern pastry creations, baking small batches daily to ensure warmth, crunch, and authentic flavor in every bite.
