About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Artisanal passion, French butter, and the warm aroma of fresh morning baking.

Our Story & Philosophy

Founded in 2026, Sweet Crumbs Bakery is dedicated to the timeless art of artisan pastry and slow-fermentation baking.

We believe the secret to unforgettable pastry lies in the purity of ingredients. That is why we exclusively use 100% French cultured butter, unbleached stone-ground wheat flour, and natural levain starters.

Whether it is our crispy signature Fruit Crombo, our golden honeycomb croissants, or our crunchy sourdough loaves, every bite is crafted to bring pure joy to your day.

Pure Ingredients

Real dairy butter, farm-fresh eggs, and natural sea salt with no artificial additives.

Baked Daily

Our ovens turn on at 04:00 AM every single morning to guarantee peak freshness.
