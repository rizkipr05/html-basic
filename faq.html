Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our opening hours, ordering, and how to keep your bakes fresh.

General questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 07:00 to 18:00, and on Sunday from 08:00 to 15:00.

Where is the bakery located?

You can find us at Jl. Roti Manis No. 8. Look for the striped awning and the smell of fresh bread.

Do you bake fresh products daily?

Yes. Our bread and pastries are baked every morning, and our cakes are made fresh for each order.

Ordering and delivery

How long do orders take?

Ready-made items are usually ready within 2 hours. Custom cakes need at least 3 days.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Choose "Delivery" on the Purchase page and enter your address. The delivery fee depends on the distance and is shown before you place your order.

Can I cancel or change an order?

You can change or cancel a regular order up to 2 hours before pickup or delivery. Custom cake orders can be changed up to 48 hours before the date.

Product care and policies

How should I store bread and cakes?

For bread, follow these steps:

  1. Let the bread cool completely before storing it.
  2. Keep it in a bread box or a paper bag at room temperature.
  3. Slice and freeze it if you need to keep it longer than 3 days.

Cakes should be kept in the fridge and taken out 30 minutes before serving.

Do you accept custom cake orders?

Yes. Tell us the flavor, size, and design on the Purchase page, and we will confirm the details with you.

Do your products contain allergens?

Many of our bakes contain wheat, milk, eggs, or nuts. Please tell us about any allergy before you order.

What are your bakery policies?

  • Custom cake orders require advance notice.
  • Product availability may vary daily.
  • Please ask us about allergens before ordering.
